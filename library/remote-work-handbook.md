# Northgate Remote Work Handbook

This handbook explains how remote work operates at Northgate. It is a sample document so you can try Folio straight away.

## Working hours and availability

Core hours are 10:00 to 15:00 in your local time zone. During core hours you are expected to be reachable on chat and to respond to direct messages within one hour. Outside core hours you may set your own schedule, as long as your weekly total matches your contract.

Teams that span more than four time zones agree a shared overlap window of at least two hours and record it in the team charter.

## Equipment and expenses

Every new employee receives a one-time home office budget of 900 euros. This covers a desk, chair, monitor and accessories. Receipts must be submitted within 60 days of purchase.

A monthly stipend of 40 euros covers internet and phone costs. It is paid automatically with salary and does not need receipts.

Laptops are replaced every three years. If a laptop is lost or stolen, report it to IT within 24 hours so the device can be locked remotely.

## Working from another country

Employees may work from another country for up to 30 days per calendar year with manager approval. Longer stays create tax and social security obligations, so they need written approval from People Operations at least six weeks in advance.

You may not work from countries on the company's restricted list, which is maintained by the Legal team.

## Meetings

Meetings should have a written agenda shared at least a day in advance. Cameras are optional for meetings with more than eight people. Every meeting ends with a short summary of decisions and owners, posted in the relevant channel.

Fridays are meeting-free for all teams except incident response.

## In-person gatherings

Each team meets in person twice a year. Travel and accommodation are paid by the company. Attendance is expected, but exceptions for health or family reasons are granted without needing to give details.

## Security

Use the company password manager and enable two-factor authentication on every work account. Public Wi-Fi is allowed only through the company VPN. Never store customer data on personal devices or personal cloud storage.
